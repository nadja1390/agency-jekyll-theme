description: Together with <a href="https://verrio.co/">Verrio</a> we are looking at how to improve the on-farm resilience of almond producers on the Iberian Peninsula. In the last decade there has been a huge increase in irrigated almond plantations. With increasing drought and the expected rise in erratic rainfall patterns, we are working with farmers and local institutions to improve water use among almond producers and implement regenerative agricultural practices to enhance on-farm resilience.
